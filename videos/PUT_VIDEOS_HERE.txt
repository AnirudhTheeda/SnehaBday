ADD YOUR VIDEOS IN THIS FOLDER
1. Copy up to 5 video files into this exact folder.
2. Supported: .mp4, .webm, .mov
3. The app sorts filenames alphabetically and uses the first five.
4. Suggested names:
   1_video.mp4
   2_video.mp4
   3_video.mp4
   4_video.mp4
   5_video.mp4

No video upload field appears on the website.
Videos autoplay muted, loop continuously, and have no visible play/pause controls.
To edit each video's caption, open birthday_data.json and edit the
video_captions list in order.
MP4 encoded with H.264 is recommended for browser compatibility.
